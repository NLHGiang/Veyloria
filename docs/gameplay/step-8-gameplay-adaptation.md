# Step 8 — Gameplay Adaptation

> **Status:** Working / Chosen Direction  
> **Purpose:** Convert Veyloria's established world, premise, themes, core fantasy, Story Bible and screenplay into a coherent gameplay architecture.

Step 8 translates narrative intent into playable rules without prematurely locking quests, maps, levels or implementation details.

## Step 8 Roadmap

1. **8.1 — Player Fantasy + Gameplay Pillars + Core Gameplay Loop**
   - Define what the player should feel they are doing.
   - Define the core gameplay pillars.
   - Define the moment-to-moment and macro gameplay loops.

2. **8.2 — Gameplay Systems & Mechanics**
   - Identify the concrete systems required to express the pillars.
   - Define system responsibilities, inputs, outputs and relationships.
   - Distinguish core systems from supporting systems.

3. **8.3 — Player Interaction Model**
   - Define how players observe, investigate, interact, choose and affect the world.
   - Establish interaction rules and feedback.

4. **8.4 — World State & Memory Architecture**
   - Translate "The World Remembers" into world-state rules.
   - Define what can remember, what can change, and how changes persist.

5. **8.5 — Progression & Unlock Structure**
   - Define understanding progression, access progression and systemic progression.
   - Avoid introducing power progression unless it serves the core fantasy.

6. **8.6 — Quest / Activity Structure**
   - Define the reusable structure of gameplay activities.
   - Establish how a problem becomes an actionable session.

7. **8.7 — Session Loop & Content Cadence**
   - Connect the 10–15 minute session philosophy to gameplay systems.
   - Define setup, action, consequence and unresolved question.

8. **8.8 — Gameplay-to-Story Integration**
   - Map story beats to mechanics without turning narrative into exposition.
   - Identify where gameplay should communicate information instead of dialogue.

9. **8.9 — Gameplay Constraints & Anti-Patterns**
   - Define what Veyloria should not become.
   - Protect the identity of the game from mechanics that do not serve its pillars.

10. **8.10 — Gameplay Adaptation Baseline**
    - Consolidate the decisions into a stable gameplay baseline.
    - Prepare the foundation for Step 9: Story → Quest → Mechanic → Level → Progression mapping.

## Step 8 North Star

Gameplay must make the player's relationship with Veyloria tangible:

> **The player acts. The world changes. The world remembers. The player later encounters what that memory became.**

The primary progression is therefore **understanding**, not raw power.

## Current Locked Baseline

- **Player Fantasy:** The Forgotten One
- **Agency Fantasy:** Today's actions become tomorrow's memories.
- **Core Conflict:** Memory ≠ Truth.
- **Signature Experience:** The player discovers that the world remembers them differently from how they remember themselves.
- **Primary Progression:** Understanding.
- **Primary Narrative/Gameplay North Star:** **The World Remembers.**

## Design Gate

Every new gameplay mechanic should justify itself against the established pillars:

- Does it encourage **Explore**?
- Does it give the player something to **Notice & Investigate**?
- Does it allow the player to **Interact & Change**?
- Does it participate in **Memory & Contradiction**?
- Does it contribute to **Consequence & World Remembers**?

If a mechanic does none of these, it should not enter the core design by default.
